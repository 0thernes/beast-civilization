## 2. Scope of the Lean Suite (2026-11-26 statement audit)

- The Lean definitions are standalone models. They are not connected to the Zig, Rust, C or PTX code that ships, and no Lean file imports or extracts that code.
- `39` of the `65` theorems are closed literal statements: spec-constant identities (FIPS-203/197 sizes, Multiboot1/2 checksums, GGUF `Q4_0` block layout), bookkeeping sums (DLL counts `B29`/`B36`/`B47`/`B57`, planted-mutant totals `B39`/`B48`/`B58`/`B63`, matrix and MoE splits) and receipt constants. Superseded counts (for example both the 11-DLL and 12-DLL sums) are still among the `65`.
- `B60` and `B65` check constants committed in `lean/LiveReceipt.lean`. CI compiles the committed file and never regenerates or diffs it, and `beast verify-all --export-lean` writes passing default values when the GPU, QEMU, AUTOPHAGE or Win32 Job Object backend is absent. These theorems do not show that any run produced the constants.
- `B54` proves only commutativity of dual-number multiplication, not the product rule its docstring names; `B55` proves commutativity of dual-number addition. Neither is evidence for the forward/reverse/symbolic AD agreement in `G27`.
- `B02` gives a left inverse of the 5-trit packing on all `243` digit tuples; surjectivity onto `0..242` is not stated.
- `B09`/`B10` ("Ring-0") model a userland root-policy rewrite guard and spend cap, not CPU ring 0. `B62` evaluates a two-input Boolean loopback rule; `B08` is `rfl` on a small enum.
- Several theorem names describe more than their statements prove: `B13_wse_spectral_contraction` proves `wMilli * x < 1000 * x` from `wMilli <= 990`; `B56_church_rosser_xor_confluence` is XOR reassociation on three `BitVec 64` values; `B46_p9_observer_independence` is `rfl`; `B35_quantum_geometric_bound_psd` is an integer inequality from a hypothesis.
- Timings, GPU kernels, DLL results and QEMU boots are empirical and outside the Lean kernel.
- No open problem is resolved; the repository states `0` of `1,900` Mega-Atlas problems retired.
