## 2. Standalone Model Boundary & Closed Ground Statement Transparency

All `81` Lean 4.35.0 declarations in `lean/` are **standalone formal models** of `BeastProofs.Spec` — no Lean file imports, extracts, or verifies the shipped Rust, Zig, C11, or PTX source code. Counting mechanically from `lean/Challenge.lean`:
- **`41` Quantified, Witness & Refutation Declarations:** `25` parameterized/quantified theorems (`B02`, `B06`–`B07`, `B09`–`B13`, `B17`–`B23`, `B25`, `B28`, `B35`, `B37`–`B38`, `B45`, `B54`–`B56`, `B64`), `8` non-vacuity witnesses (`NV01`–`NV08`), and `8` formal negations (`AT01`–`AT08`). Specifically:
  - `B02` proves `pack5Trits < 243` and left-inverse `unpack5Trits (pack5Trits t) = t` across all 243 `Fin 3` tuples (full surjectivity onto `Fin 243` is checked exhaustively in `scripts/verify_independent_oracle.py` and `scripts/check_sanitizers.sh`).
  - `B09`–`B10` model a userland root-policy rewrite record (`RootState`), not CPU ring 0.
  - `B54`–`B55` prove commutativity of `DualInt` multiplication and addition only; floating-point tri-mode AD agreement (`max_err = 0.00e0`) is an empirical verification result (`G27` + Python oracle).
- **`40` Closed Ground & Committed Receipt-Constant Checks:** Exact spec sizes, checksums, bookkeeping sums, constant predicates (`B08`, `B43`, `B46`, `B62`), and committed receipt-constant consistency checks (`B60`, `B65`). `beast verify-all --export-lean` writes `0` whenever a hardware backend is absent (zero passing-default substitution), and `B60`/`B65` check consistency of committed constants in `LiveReceiptData.lean` rather than proving inside Lean that external silicon ran.
